---
title: "View invoices"
---

To view your invoices for Data Fetcher, you'll need to follow these steps:

1. Sign in to your account at https://datafetcher.com/sign-in
2. Make sure the correct workspace is selected in the top-left dropdown.
3. Click **Settings** in the top menu.
4. Click **Billing** in the left-hand sidebar.
5. Under **Invoices**, click **View invoices**.
6. This will redirect you to Stripe (Data Fetcher's payment processor).
7. Once in Stripe, you can find your complete invoice history under the **INVOICE HISTORY** section.
8. From there, you can view and download any invoices you need.
