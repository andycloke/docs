---
title: "Update payment card"
---

You can update the payment card used for your Data Fetcher workspace by following these steps:

1. Sign in to your account at https://datafetcher.com/sign-in.
2. Make sure the correct workspace is selected in the top-left dropdown.
3. Click **Settings** in the top menu.
4. Click **Billing** in the left-hand sidebar.
5. Under **Payment & invoices**, click **Update card**.
6. This will redirect you to Stripe (Data Fetcher's payment processor).
7. Click **\+ Add payment method**.
8. Enter your card details.
9. Ensure **Use as default payment method** is selected.
10. Click **Add** to save your new payment card.
