---
title: "Update billing information"
---

You can update the billing name, address and email address that appear on your Data Fetcher invoices by following these steps:

1. Sign in to your account at https://datafetcher.com/sign-in.
2. Make sure the correct workspace is selected in the top-left dropdown.
3. Click **Settings** in the top menu.
4. Click **Billing** in the left-hand sidebar.
5. Under **Payment information**, click **Update billing info**.
6. This will redirect you to Stripe (Data Fetcher's payment processor).
7. Under the **BILLING INFORMATION** section, click **Update information**.
8. Make your changes to the billing name, address and/or email address.
9. Click **Save** to apply your changes.

Note that these changes will only apply to future invoices. It's not possible to update information on invoices that have already been issued.
